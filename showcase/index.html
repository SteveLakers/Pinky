Research project · not investment advice

Pinky tries to pick the stocks
that beat the S&P 500.

It is a machine-learning model that reads years of price history and, once a month, ranks every stock it follows. It buys the top 10 and holds them until the next month. It leans toward tech and tries to spot big winners early — but it still buys energy, industrials and other sectors when they rank best.

How it works

Six steps, repeated every month

Nothing here is hand-picked. The model only ever sees the past when it makes a choice.

01Price dataDaily prices for the stocks we follow, plus the S&P 500.
02FeaturesTurn raw prices into signals: trend, volatility, strength vs the market.
03Walk-forward trainingTrain on the past, test on the next unseen stretch. Repeat. Never peek ahead.
04RankingEach stock gets a score: how much it should beat the index over the next year.
05Top-10 portfolioBuy the 10 best, equal amounts, max 60% from any one sector.
06Costs & scoringCharge trading costs, then compare honestly against the S&P 500.

What's next

Fix the data first, then get smarter

Phase 1 · in progress

Honest evaluation

  • Buy proper historical data (Sharadar), including companies that went bust or got bought.
  • Widen from 128 hand-picked names to ~2,500–4,000 liquid US stocks, as they existed at the time.
  • Re-score today's model to get a baseline we can trust.
Phase 2 · next

Look at the companies

  • Growth and revenue acceleration — first, and most important.
  • Margins, earnings reactions, R&D spending and returns on capital.
  • Quality and safety checks, plus insider buying.
  • Added one batch at a time; anything that doesn't beat the baseline gets dropped.
Phase 3 · only if it works

Paper trading

  • Publish a monthly picks report.
  • Track it live with fake money for a long stretch.
  • Real money only after that, if ever.
