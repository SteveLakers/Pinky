Research project · not investment advice

Pinky tries to pick the stocks
that beat the S&P 500.

Once a month it ranks the liquid US stocks and buys the best-ranked ones. The aim is to spot big winners early. Tech is the sector we like most, but the model can buy any sector.

How it works

Six steps, repeated every month

Nothing here is hand-picked. The model only ever sees the past when it makes a choice.

01Honest dataDaily prices for every US stock, including companies that later went bust or were bought.
02Liquid universe
03FeaturesPrice signals only, so far: trend, momentum, volatility, volume.
04Walk-forwardTrain on the past, test on the next unseen year. Repeat. Never peek ahead.
05PortfolioBuy the top-ranked stocks in equal amounts, max 60% per sector. Keep a holding while it stays near the top.
06Costs & scoringCharge trading costs, then compare with the S&P 500 and with owning every stock.
